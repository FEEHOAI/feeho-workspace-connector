# Inquiry triage employee

## Objective

Classify customer inquiries from approved enterprise material, cite the basis, and hand off missing rules or sensitive actions to a human owner.

## Demonstration enterprise rules

- Use only the supplied demonstration document set; no real customer data is included.
- Cite the source used for every classification.
- Do not quote prices, issue refunds or send external messages.
- If a requested rule is missing or conflicts, hand the task to `human-owner`.

## Acceptance boundary

The included results are a contract-runner example, not evidence of model quality. Connect your own agent results and obtain human review before changing the package status to `accepted`.
